Chocolate Brownies

Recipe for Delicious Chocolate Brownies + Easy to make!!!

Ingredients

  • 185g Unsalted Butter
  • 185g Dark Chocolate
  • 85g Plain Flour
  • 40g Cocoa Powder
  • 50g White Chocolate
  • 50g Milk Chocolate
  • 3 Large Eggs
  • 275g golden Caster Sugar

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 175 Celsius
  2. In a medium, heatproof bowl, combine the butter and the chopped dark chocolate.
  3. Melt them together using a double boiler or in the microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring until completely smooth.
  4. Set aside to cool slightly for about $5$-$10$ minutes. (It should be warm, but not hot enough to cook the eggs.)
  5. In a large bowl, or directly into the slightly cooled chocolate mixture, whisk in the granulated sugar until well combined.
  6. Add the eggs (preferably room temperature) and vanilla extract. Whisk vigorously for about $1$ minute until the mixture becomes thicker and glossy. This step is key to achieving that desirable crackly crust!
  7. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, and salt.
  8. DO NOT OVERMIX. Stop stirring as soon as the last streaks of flour disappear. Overmixing develops gluten and can make your brownies tough or cakey.
  9. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes.
  10. Check for doneness: Fudgy brownies are done when the edges look set and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with moist, fudgy crumbs attached, but not wet batter. If the toothpick comes out clean, they are overdone.
  11. Remove the pan from the oven and place it on a wire rack.
