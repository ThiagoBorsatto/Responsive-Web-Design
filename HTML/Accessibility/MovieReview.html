Mad Max: Fury Road

Is a high-octane, post-apocalyptic action film where loner Max Rockatansky (Tom Hardy) reluctantly joins Imperator Furiosa (Charlize Theron) to escape a tyrannical cult leader, Immortan Joe. They flee across a desert wasteland in a heavily armored "War Rig," protecting Joe’s five wives while battling his army.
